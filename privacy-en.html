HAJIKIST Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

HAJIKIST ("the App") is an iOS puzzle game provided by an individual developer ("the Developer"). This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information stored by the App

The App stores the following information only on your device. It is never sent to the Developer's servers.

  • Play records (games played, scores, chains, titles, etc.)
  • Settings (name, language, sound, vibration, control buttons, etc.)
  • The purchase state of the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase

2. Information shared with your opponent in battles

In online battles (Game Center) and nearby battles (local networking), the following is sent to your opponent's device:

  • The name you set (or your device name if none is set)
  • The title you are using
  • Your board and score during the match

This information is used only to run the match and is never collected or stored by the Developer. Apple's privacy policy applies to the use of Game Center.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), device information, approximate location and app usage data.

  • Ad tracking happens only if you allow it in the iOS "Allow Tracking" dialog. The App works without permission; in that case non-personalized ads are shown.
  • You can change the permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Google's advertising policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Purchasing "Remove Ads" stops all ads from being shown.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. The Developer never receives payment card details. Purchases can be restored from Settings in the App.

5. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Voice

The App's voice lines are synthesized with VOICEVOX (Shikoku Metan). Credit: VOICEVOX:四国めたん
